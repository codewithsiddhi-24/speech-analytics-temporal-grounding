Data quality & reference corpus

Uploaded recordings are compared automatically with the combined reference recordings. Source recordings remain pending human review; deviation candidates are not verified speech flaws.

Model performance

Evaluation status

Accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and boundary overlap are unavailable. No manually verified correct/incorrect timestamp labels exist for a held-out evaluation set. An 80–85% target cannot honestly be asserted from unlabeled data.

Current model

Isolation Forest · multi-recording reference deviation

Fitted on word intervals from every available source recording. Features include alignment score, word duration and neighboring gaps, local energy/voicing/centroid changes, and zero-crossing rate. The source set is not human-verified clean ground truth.

What is needed for accuracy

  1. Annotate timestamps as correct or incorrect using audio review.
  2. Keep recordings/speakers separate between training and held-out test sets.
  3. Report precision, recall, F1, and timestamp-boundary error on that held-out set.
  4. Tune only on training/validation data, then measure test performance once.

Candidate counts are prioritization totals, not correctness metrics.

Transparent methodology

How It Works

Processing stages and the limits of each output.

01

Audio input

Local uploads accept 16-bit PCM WAV and are analyzed on this computer. Uploaded samples are removed after processing.

02

Transcript & timestamps

The English small.en speech recognizer returns word-level timestamps and confidence cues. These are not forced-alignment confidence scores.

03

Acoustic cues

Frame energy and a spectrogram are computed from the uploaded samples. Gaps are timed between recognized words.

04

Review cues

Low ASR confidence can mean unclear speech, but noise and accents can also lower it. Energy cannot reliably tell breath from silence.

05

Reference comparison

Word timing and acoustic features are scored automatically against the combined reference recordings. Missing forced-alignment scores are imputed for uploads.

06

Temporal grounding

Words, detected gaps, and low-energy intervals are shown with timestamps, transcript context, and audio playback. Deviation candidates prioritize review; they are not validated flaw predictions.

Event meanings & caveats

Reference-deviation candidate
A word interval unusual relative to the combined source corpus; review needed, not a confirmed flaw.

Low-energy interval
An acoustic cue that may be a natural pause, breath, or silence. This detector cannot reliably identify which.

Hesitation, rushing, dragging, monotone, mumbling: there are no human-verified labels for these natural flaw types. Upload-time low ASR confidence is only a cue to listen, not a diagnosis.

Severity and review status: not provided by the model; no confirmed/dismissed workflow is claimed.
